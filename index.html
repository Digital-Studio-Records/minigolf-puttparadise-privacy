Mini Golf Putt Paradise Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

In short

  • Putt Paradise has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress, scores, coins and settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads.
  • You can limit ad personalization in your device settings and, where the law requires it, in the consent message the game shows.

1Who we are

Mini Golf Putt Paradise ("Putt Paradise") is a mini golf game made by Digital Studio Records ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Mini Golf Putt Paradise app on Android and any other platform where we release it.

2Information saved on your device

To remember your game, Putt Paradise saves the following on your device only:

  • Which holes you have opened and beaten, your best score on each hole, and your best rounds
  • Your coins, achievements, and progress on daily and weekly challenges
  • Your music and sound settings

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it. Pass And Play mode stores scores only for the round being played and does not record players' names.

3Advertising

Putt Paradise is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads appear between some holes, and you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to get extra strokes or bonus coins. To serve these ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

InformationUsed for
Your device's advertising IDShowing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring results and, if you allow it, personalizing ads
IP address and the general location it suggests (for example, your country)Choosing ads suitable for your region and preventing fraud
Device and app details (model, operating system, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device
How you interact with ads (views, taps, whether a rewarded ad finished)Giving rewards, measuring ads and preventing fraud
Crash and diagnostic information from the ad softwareKeeping the ads working

We do not receive this information ourselves; it is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not sell your personal information. Ads shown in the game may be personalized by Google unless you choose otherwise, as described below.

4Your choices

  • Consent message. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another place where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the game's settings.
  • Ad personalization on Android. Open your device's Settings, go to Google, then Ads, and turn off ad personalization or delete your advertising ID. You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you.
  • Deleting game data. Uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings to remove everything the game saved.

5Children

Putt Paradise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help make sure it is removed.

6Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or state privacy laws in the United States), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are best handled by Google for ad data, or by deleting the app's data on your device. You can still contact us and we will help.

7Security and retention

Game data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. Information collected for ads is kept by Google for the periods described in its policies. All connections the ad software makes are encrypted.

8Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, adding in-app purchases or online features), we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.

9Contact us

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email Digital Studio Records at support@digitalstudiorecords.com.
